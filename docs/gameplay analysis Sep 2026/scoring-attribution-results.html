These are observed reward origins. They do not measure how much score would disappear without your shooting, upgrade choices or active timing.

How credit travels

1 · OriginManual shot, upgrade system or impact creates a damaging effect.
2 · Kill + chainFinal blow owns the kill. Descendants retain their initiating source, even after the root expires.
3 · Shared comboThe current global multiplier boosts every kill source. Manual and flare roots can raise or reset it.
4 · Other awardsRoot multi-kill awards and wave/building bonuses add points separately.

An automated kill can benefit from a combo built by manual shots. Assigning that uplift to its final kill source is accounting, not a causal claim about player skill.

Who received its extra points?

The report separates base kill value from uplift. It does not assign each later multiplier point to an earlier shot or flare: their effects overlap through the shared combo state.

Shots, kills and the awkward counters

The game's playerCaused flag includes flare explosions. Treating it as “manual shot” would count flare multi-kills and combo changes as shooting performance.

No kills had damaging contributors from different sources in these recordings; the observer's assist case was verified separately. “Kill-producing shot” uses observed final kills, including chains. It does not estimate a save or an avoided future hit.

What this establishes

  • Final score combines shooting, upgrade-system kills and survival bonuses. It is not a pure shooting measure.
  • Manual and flare roots affect a multiplier that also rewards the other systems. Final-blow ownership alone misses that relationship.
  • Multi-kill awards belong to several sources, including enemy-impact explosions. The game's multi-shot counter is narrower, but still includes flare roots.
  • Score attribution cannot answer whether late waves leave meaningful timing decisions. That remains a separate opportunity/pressure study.

The earlier 53.2% bonus share used completed-wave earnings only. This report's 47.8% uses complete run totals, including terminal-wave earnings. Both describe the same corpus with different denominators.

Method, reproduction and limitations
  • Gameplay source files were transformed only in memory for an analysis bundle. No game rules, reward values, RNG calls or action ordering were changed. Exact-count source replacements and AST insertions are recorded in the local instrumentation inventory.
  • Both bundles expose identical read-only replay-consumption metadata. Full sanitized replay-anchor state, including Sets/Maps, RNG state and action/checkpoint consumption, is hashed every 120 ticks, at recorded checkpoint ticks, and at observed state/shop/bonus boundaries. This is sampled equivalence, not an every-tick full-state comparison.
  • Reward sums, destroyed counts, fired shots and multi-shot counters reconcile per wave and per run. All actions and checkpoints are consumed, final ticks are exact, and maintained recap comparison matches stored summaries.
  • Controlled cases cover player/system/impact chains after root removal, mixed-source damage assists, incremental multi-kill awards, actual flare turncoat payoff, unscored neutralization and successful shot identity. Source-labelled synthetic explosions test lineage; they do not independently exercise every system's firing routine.
  • All 26 recordings are this player's iOS draft-mode runs across eight builds, ending in Burj destruction on waves 2–10. There is no wave-11 recording, player comparison or counterfactual simulation. No paid spending or friendly-fire penalties occurred.
  • Sources follow real damaging calls, not distance or threat altitude. Per-cast lineage for active systems is not established. Shots without a logged resolution can include effects cleared at a boundary or still unresolved at the recording end.
  • Private replay payloads and detailed event logs stay under operator-results/scoring-study-20260915/attribution/. This HTML embeds only study labels and summarized results.

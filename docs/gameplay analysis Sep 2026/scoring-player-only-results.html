Points for the player,
not the turrets.

The same 26 recorded runs, rescored under two proposed rules. Rule 1: only player-initiated actions (interceptor shots, F-15, EMP, flares) earn kill points. Automated defenses earn nothing, apart from multi-kill bonuses. Rule 2, tested on its own and combined with rule 1: the combo caps at ×5 and pays a cash-out bonus on the next hit, then resets.

This is an exact rescoring, not a simulation

All 26 recordings were played in draft mode: wave-end picks are free and score never feeds back into the game. Every recorded shot, kill, pick and outcome therefore stays valid, and each number below is exactly what that run would have scored under the rule. What the recordings cannot show is how players would adapt to the new incentives.

Rules and bonus sizes were fixed in the method note before any result was calculated. Today's rules reproduce all 1,663,424 recorded points, every wave total and the recorded multiplier on all 5,308 kills.

Where today's points come from

Survival bonuses are nearly half of all points. Automation and impact kills are 12.3%. Of automation's kill points, 64% came from the player's combo multiplier: a player's streak was multiplying Patriot and Hornet kills.

Survival: waves cleared and buildings standing

Two bonuses are paid only when a wave is completed; the wave that ends a run pays neither.

  • Wave clear: 250 × wave number. Pure survival, 171,000 points (10.3% of all points).
  • Buildings: 100 × buildings standing × wave number. The city starts with 10 buildings and never rebuilds them. 624,500 points (37.5%).

With every building standing, the building bonus would have paid 684,000. Building losses cost only 59,500 points (3.6% of all points), so 91% of the building bonus is paid simply for surviving the wave. In practice the building bonus is a second, larger wave-survival bonus: protecting buildings moves the score very little.

Both rules leave these bonuses untouched, so their share grows: 47.8% of all points today, 54.5% under rule 1, 56.7% with cash-out 350 and 55.5% with cash-out 500.

Rule 1: automation earns nothing

Pooled, the rule removes 204,694 points (−12.3%); the median run loses −11.6%. The hardest-hit runs were short and relied on automation: run-002 (−39%, ended wave 5, Patriot + Hornets), run-001 (−30%, ended wave 2, Hornets), run-018 (−29%, ended wave 5, Hornets + Iron Beam + Roadrunner). The 15 runs that reached wave 8 or later lose 7.5–15.5%.

Does the ranking change?

Barely, on final totals: how far a run got decides most of its score, through survival bonuses and more waves of kills. Comparing runs within the same wave is more sensitive: rank agreement with today ranges from 0.72 to 1.00 across waves.

Rule 2: cap ×5 and cash out

How the combo behaves today

23 of 26 runs fired at ×10. Only 11% of shots were fired at ×10, yet 28% of player kill points were earned there. ×10 is an early-game state: 33% of shots in waves 2–4, 2% from wave 5 on. Later waves break the streak because empty shots climb from 9% in wave 1 to 50% from wave 5.

Two resets look unfair under either combo rule. In 258 cases the player's combo reset after automation killed the shot's target first. In 97 cases a flare explosion with no kills counted as a player miss (40 of them from ×2 or higher), because flare explosions feed the combo like interceptor shots.

Who pays for the cash-out

Across the corpus there are 191 cash-outs (7.3 per run). On top of rule 1, cash-out 350 changes a run by −16.0% to +0.2%. The loss tracks how much of a run's points came at ×10 (ρ -0.75): run-009 earned 88% at ×10 and loses 16.0%; run-025 earned 58% at ×10 and loses 12.1%; run-015 earned 36% at ×10 and loses 10.2%. The 20 runs that earned under 30% of their player points at ×10 lose at most 6.2% at 350; 18 of them gain at 700.

What this suggests

  • Rule 1 is a clean, moderate cut. It removes 12.3% of points, mostly from late waves and automation-heavy builds, and stops the player's combo from multiplying turret kills. It leaves the leaderboard order almost intact.
  • Decide whether the multi-kill exception stays. It keeps 126,500 points flowing to automation, mostly Patriot and Hornets. It keeps the DOUBLE/TRIPLE toasts consistent, but it is 7.6% of all points.
  • The cash-out at 350 penalizes the longest streaks, which are the most consistent shooting in these recordings. If the aim is a rhythm of short streaks without penalizing long ones, the bonus needs to be about 700 or more, or it should grow with each consecutive cash-out.
  • Consider not resetting the combo on non-misses: when automation killed the target first, or when a flare hit nothing. These are 355 of 1,344 resets, and the player could not prevent them.
  • Rescoring cannot show how people will play under a new combo. A playtest behind a debug toggle is the next honest step.

Verification and reproduction

Today's rules, replayed from the ledgers, reproduce all 26 run totals, all 194 wave totals (26 terminal), every combo transition and the recorded multiplier on all 5,308 kills. Independent Python agrees on 2,328 wave × scenario values, 312 rank checks, per-source totals and all 672 streaks. Ten hand-worked rule cases cover ownership, multi-kills, cash-out timing, resets and wave carry-over. SHA-256 digests confirm all 55 consumed inputs are unchanged.

Judge the choice, not just the kill

The focal shot or withholding interval begins near 2 seconds. Look at the lead-in and outcome before deciding.

  • Productive: placement or timing usefully catches threats.
  • Wasteful: the choice appears avoidably unproductive.
  • Sensible withholding: allowing existing defenses to work looks reasonable.
  • Ambiguous: the outcome alone does not settle the choice.

A threat disappearing first does not prove a bad shot. A low interception does not prove a save.

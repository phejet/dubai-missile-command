What we can say now

  • Manual shots are frequently productive. 2,757 manual-origin kills came from 3,394 shots, including chain descendants. A shot can hit several threats.
  • Empty shots have different histories. Some lose intended targets to other defenses or another manual shot. Others begin with no intended target because the player is aiming ahead.
  • Withholding is sometimes followed by a good local outcome. Existing defenses can remove threats while the player has firing capacity available. Observing that outcome does not establish the player's intent.
  • Your criterion is protecting targets. You corrected clip 02 to Productive and emphasized preventing threats from reaching their targets. Dense scenes make individual-shot judgments difficult. Shot efficiency remains descriptive, not a validated skill score.

Opportunity measurement limits

Availability is projected on a cloned charge state before each recorded action tick, preserving simulation state. We exclude shops, wave-complete intervals and post-destruction animation. A threat being present does not establish a viable or useful aim point; an upgrade being owned does not prove a lane was covered. Withholding windows are outcome-selected examples, not evidence that all non-shooting time was wise.

Dubai Missile Command

Privacy policy

Effective 2 September 2026

The short version

Playtest uploads are optional and off until you consent in the app. We use uploaded runs to improve game balance, diagnose problems, and power replay links you explicitly share. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.

What is collected

When playtest uploads are enabled, a completed-run upload can include:

  • a random app-install label and an Apple App Attest credential identifier used for security;
  • app build, app flavor, platform, input type, and server receipt time;
  • score, wave, outcome, play time, accuracy, kills, upgrades, health, and other run statistics;
  • the replay action log needed to reproduce the run; and
  • one optional emoji selected on the Run Recap screen.

A diagnostic report is separate and deliberate. It can include recent in-app diagnostic events, screen and device-display characteristics, a replay or partial replay, and any note included with that report. Ordinary automatic run uploads never include diagnostic events or free-text notes.

The service processes an IP address transiently for abuse rate-limiting, but does not store it in the run or diagnostic records. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, or payment information.

Why it is used

  • Compare gameplay balance and outcomes across app builds.
  • Investigate bugs, crashes, replay divergence, and performance problems.
  • Authenticate genuine app installs and prevent abusive or forged submissions.
  • Create a public replay only when you choose Share Run.

Retention

  • Compact gameplay summaries and emoji feedback: 365 days from server receipt.
  • Full replay objects and public share mappings: 270 days from server receipt.
  • Diagnostic reports, diagnostic objects, display names, and free-text notes: 90 days.
  • Manual-deletion manifests are removed on completion. A job blocked before deletion is purged after 30 days; a partially completed job retains its recovery manifest only until verified completion, when only hashed audit references and counts remain.
  • Active App Attest security credentials remain while active. After revocation, the minimum pseudonymous key hash, revoked status, and audit timestamps remain for the life of the capture service so the revoked key cannot re-enroll.

Expired replays and public links stop working at the stated boundary even if scheduled physical cleanup runs late. A summary may remain after its replay has expired.

Your choices

You can disable Playtest Uploads or Auto-upload Runs from Options. Disabling stops future uploads and clears the local retry queue; it does not erase material already received. Reinstalling the app replaces its random install label rather than restoring it from Keychain.

To request deletion of an uploaded run or all uploads associated with a known test install, email phejet@gmail.com. Include a shared-run link, approximate play date and build, or another detail agreed with the tester operator. Do not post private identifiers in a public issue. Requests are verified manually while the tester cohort is small, then removed from both the database and object storage.

Deleting gameplay data does not silently delete the App Attest revocation record. If you also want future submissions from that app credential stopped, request credential revocation as part of the same message.

Sharing and service providers

Uploaded data is stored using Cloudflare Workers, D1, and R2. A Share Run link makes only that replay and its score, wave, outcome, and build publicly accessible until expiry. Private install labels, credentials, diagnostic notes, and other operator fields are never included in the public replay response.

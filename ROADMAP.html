RM-09 Building targeting and impact audit Analyzed Explain why surrounding buildings survive and identify genuine missed impacts.

Scope: inventory all available replays; audit target selection, trajectories, collision and destruction for city buildings, excluding the Burj. Separate sites and launchers.

Exit: verified corpus coverage, targeting percentages with explicit denominators, reconciled threat outcomes and building losses, trajectory evidence for suspicious misses, and minimal reproductions for confirmed defects. Separate interception and prior target loss from bugs.
